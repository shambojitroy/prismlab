Laboratory Infrastructure • Research Support

Instruments and Facilities

01

Inverted / Research Microscope

A research microscope for high-quality visualization and characterization of biological samples, cells, tissues, and microstructures. It supports routine microscopy and detailed imaging workflows used across the lab’s biomaterials and cell-based research.

02

Hot Plate Magnetic Stirrer and Analytical Balance

Essential sample-preparation and formulation equipment. The magnetic stirrer provides controlled heating and stirring for solution preparation and experimental workflows, while the analytical balance enables precise measurement of reagents and samples.

03

Biosafety Cabinet

A controlled work enclosure designed for safe handling of biological materials. It provides a protected workspace and helps minimize contamination during cell culture and other aseptic laboratory procedures.

04

Refrigerated Centrifuge

A temperature-controlled storage facility for reagents, buffers, biological materials, and other laboratory supplies that require refrigerated conditions. It supports organized and reliable short- to medium-term sample storage.

05

Shaker Incubator

A temperature-controlled incubation system with orbital shaking. It is useful for controlled culture and mixing applications where stable temperature and agitation are required for reproducible experimental conditions.

06

Protein Gel Electrophoresis Machine

A laboratory system used to separate proteins according to their size and charge through gel electrophoresis. It supports protein analysis, characterization, and research workflows involving electrophoretic separation of biological samples.

07

Probe Sonicator (Homogenizer)

Required for homogenizing cells, tissues and crystals (with temporal control) for isolation of proteins, peptides and preparation of printing inks. Supports the materials and peptide synthesis projects.
